WR Editor Fix - Music (WR Automate Beat Sync)

Put your songs in this folder: mp3, wav, m4a, flac or wma.

In the editor: marker menu -> WR Options -> WR Automate -> Music -> pick a song.
WR Automate then puts its markers on the beat of that song, the video is as long
as the song section you pick, and the song plays in the editor when you press Play.
New songs show up right away (Music page -> Song -> Enter reads the folder again).

Music\Edits\ is made by WR Automate: after every Beat Sync run it writes an edit
sheet (.txt + .json) with the song section and every marker's song time - use it to
put the song in the right place in your video editor. The export itself has no song
in it.
